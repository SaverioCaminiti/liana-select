liana-select

Swing through a tree of options and select one in a single, swift gesture.

Press on a category, slide right into its column, keep going, and release on a dish. Or tap one level at a time. Slide back left to change an earlier choice. With the keyboard: Tab into a select, move with the arrow keys, confirm with Enter.
